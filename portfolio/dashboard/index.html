Which companies moved the portfolio

Each company's contribution to the total change, in percentage points of the starting value: your exposure at the start × the company's price change in euros. Shows the 10 biggest lifts and drags. The two grey bars hold the rest, so all bars add up to the total %.

Every company

Your exposure = value of your position in each ETF × the company's share of that ETF, added up over every ETF that holds it, at the latest close. Price % is the company's own move in euros. Click a column to sort.

Holdings come from Yahoo Finance's top-10 lists. Where an ETF's own listing has no data, the list comes from another fund tracking the same index, named in each ETF's detail. For swap-based ETFs this shows the index you get the returns of, not the basket the fund legally owns. Company prices are each market's own closes converted to euros at Yahoo's exchange rates; US markets close after the European ETFs, so part of the timing difference lands in the "rest" bar. Bond ETFs are left out: Yahoo publishes no bond holdings for them. The commodity ETF (LYTR) holds no companies.

How it is worked out: change in € = quantity held at the start × (latest close − start close). Change in % = total change in € ÷ portfolio value at the start. Contribution = a holding's change in € ÷ portfolio value at the start, so the contributions add up to the total %. Buys and sells = latest value − start value − change from prices.

Prices are daily closing prices in EUR from Yahoo Finance, collected every weekday morning. Quantities come from your Trading 212 positions on 2 Nov 2026. 79U0 is priced on the Munich exchange, where it trades thinly, so its price can lag. A price marked "last known" could not be fetched that day and is carried forward.
